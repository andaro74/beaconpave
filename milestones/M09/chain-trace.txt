MER-AI-0001  AI-authored editorial copy must carry a visible disclosure
  source     regulation - Fictional State of Jefferson AI Disclosure Act, 2026 (demo artifact)
  owner      legal-sp        status enforced        review_by 2026-10-01
  scope      legal-sp 2026-09-10
               AI-authored editorial copy the bound service publishes to
               viewers about catalog titles — previews, tile and home-screen
               blurbs, and publishable summaries of what a title is and who
               may watch it. The test is authorship and publication, not
               grammatical mood: a preview requested as a question is in
               scope and a factual entitlement answer requested as a command
               is not. **Not narrowed by brand.** An earlier draft of this
               field read "that Meridian Sports publishes", which was a
               SECOND narrowing — of the surface — smuggled into a field
               whose whole justification is the recap measurement below, and
               given no revival condition of its own. A disclosure act does
               not stop at a brand boundary; the rule binds to the service,
               and which brands that service carries is the manifest's to
               say.
  excludes
               AI-generated recaps — editorial copy narrating the outcome of
               an event. Not retired and not disclaimed: the obligation is
               sound and the bound service has no instance of it, because
               the catalog carries no outcomes and the golden set forbids
               the agent inventing one.
  revives
               Either: the catalog gaining an outcome field for a title, or
               a service that authors post-event copy joining the registry
               as a caller. The first to land re-opens the recap sense as a
               scope decision on this seat's key, and the eval pack gains
               its recap half in that milestone. **The Meridian News surface
               is NOT a revival condition, because it is not a future
               event.** An earlier draft named it as one, "arriving at M10"
               — and `data/catalog.json` already carries `t002 North Haven
               Nightly` and `t004 The Port William Report` at `brand:
               meridian-news`, `tools/catalog-search` will query that brand,
               and golden case `brand-020` already asks about one of them.
               News copy is in scope today under `covers`; what is excluded
               is the recap SENSE, for every brand alike.
  limit      owed to tool-owner, legal-sp — the milestone that touches the publish path
               An eval-pack disposition is not a publish-class disposition.
               The L3 pack asserts `ai_disclosure` on the agent's ANSWER.
               The action that puts editorial copy in front of viewers is
               `publish-highlight`, whose input carries `ai_generated` — not
               in `required`, defaulting true, and measurably flippable to
               false with every semantic check green (only four byte digests
               move, and the PR that moves them re-pins them). There is no
               disclosure-TEXT field on the publish input at all, so the
               string this rule is about has no route to the approver, who
               sees a boolean.
  limit      owed to legal-sp, ai-quality — the milestone that wires a disclosure judge axis, if one is ever owed
               The control reads the disclosure's WORDS, not its sense. It
               requires one of a listed set of phrasings as whole words —
               after round 2 measured that a substring match passed
               "Available now." and an explicit denial of AI authorship, and
               failed five correct disclosures. Word boundaries fix those.
               What no deterministic check of this shape can fix is
               NEGATION: a field reading "written by a human, not by AI"
               contains the token and would pass. That is a judge's
               question, and M09 wires no judge axis for disclosure on
               purpose (CLAUDE.md's deterministic-first rule, and the frozen
               instrument must not move inside the disposition). It is
               bounded here rather than left implicit: the fix's PR writes
               the disclosure sentence itself, so the pack scores a known
               sentence rather than adversarial text, and a negated
               disclosure is a finding about the fix rather than a hole the
               model can walk through.
