## What broke?

**1. The claim delegated its intervention to a site nobody had located, and the owning
document had already located it.** ADR-078 decision 2 ran every falsifier's reader over
M09's record before the claim was written. It did not read the tool plane's own record
of why these cases fail. SPEC/02:551 named `recommend-003`, `recommend-013`,
`recommend-014` and `multi-023` as losses on *contract-cannot-express*, and :634-641
named the fix as a semver change. Twelve milestones later, a claim was pre-registered
on changing it inside one milestone.

**2. Decision 5's line was drawn for the deliverables and never applied to the claim.**
ADR-078:277 separates a tier or a gate, which *"refuses committed runs on the scorer's
side only"*, from the rename, which refuses them on the model's side. The contract change
was never placed on either side of that line. SPEC/09c constraint 7 then prepared
two-key rules for `search.py` and `server.py` and none for the contract. The operator's
PR 2 ruling had the change land in PR 3 between the runs. The blast radius was measured
only at step 4 of the third PR 2 brief.

**3. The diagnosis stopped twice before it named anything.**
- **First stop:** the trajectories, read at F1's width, showed three mechanisms, and F1
  says *"the site"*, singular. That classification was run from the session's scratchpad
  and **is not committed**. It is the third time in two milestones that a reading a
  decision rested on did not reach the repository. Nothing in this journal rests on it,
  except the disclosure that it was read.
- **Second stop:** a rule was drafted that would have booked the zero-row returns as a
  retrieval defect in `search.py`. SPEC/02:634 says *"must not be booked as one"*. It was
  stopped before it was committed.

**4. The per-PR brief moved the derivation executors out of PR 2.** ADR-078 decisions 3
and 4 put them in PR 2, reviewed by the spare before PR 3. The brief moved them to PR 4,
which would have left the cold review nothing to review. It is moot now, because neither
was built. It is recorded because no ADR records it.

**5. F4's wording is ambiguous, and the ambiguity is inherited.** SPEC/09c:66 says *"a
case outside the seven that failed **0 of 3** on control passes by majority"*. Read
literally, failing 0 of 3 is passing 3 of 3. SPEC/09:57 has the same words, and
`milestones/M09/f4.py:183` executed them as *all three FAIL*. Nothing was read on it
here, so nothing was mis-read.
